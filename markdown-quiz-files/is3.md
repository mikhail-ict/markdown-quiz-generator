# 🧩 Knowledge check: Knowledge Management and Business Intelligence

This is a Knowledge Check for the topic: **Knowledge management and business intelligence**. It is intended to be a quick check of your knowledge and understanding of the key points of the topic to help support your studies. It does not count towards your assessment.

There are ten questions which are a mixture of multiple-choice and true/false questions. There is no time limit, and you can have as many attempts as you want.

Select all the correct answers and then select **Check**.

---
1. Which of the following is true about knowledge management? (Select three)
    - [x] It is the processes needed to generate, capture, codify, and transfer knowledge across the organisation
    - [x] Allows individuals to own their creativity
    - [ ] Makes technology less relevant to avoid errors
    - [x] Helps to achieve competitive advantage

2. Consider the pamphlet that you get inside a standard box of non-prescription medicines for headache or pain. Which of the following is true? Is this an example of…
    - [ ] Data
    - [x] Information
    - [ ] Knowledge
    - [ ] Data, information, and knowledge (all three options)

3. Business intelligence focuses not only on enterprise data, but on all organisational knowledge. True or false?
    - [ ] True
    - [x] False

4. Complete the sentence: “[…] is a collection of facts or symbols in a raw state.”
    - [ ] Wisdom
    - [ ] Information
    - [ ] Knowledge
    - [x] Data

5. In the SECI model, tacit to tacit is also identified as:
    - [x] Socialisation
    - [ ] Externalisation
    - [ ] Internalisation
    - [ ] Combination

6. In the SECI model, explicit to explicit is also identified as:
    - [ ] Socialisation
    - [ ] Externalisation
    - [ ] Internalisation
    - [x] Combination

7. Which of these is the correct term for developing knowledge management practices as part of everyday working practice?
    - [ ] Conscious competence
    - [x] Conscious development
    - [ ] Unconscious competence
    - [ ] Competitive consciousness

8. Complete the sentence: “A conversation with a colleague that informs you of new knowledge relating to your job role is an example of developing […]”.
    - [ ] Implicit knowledge
    - [ ] General knowledge
    - [x] Tacit knowledge
    - [ ] Explicit knowledge

9. Complete the sentence: “A schedule sent out via email of training that you need to complete as part of your onboarding process is an example of […]”.
    - [ ] Implicit knowledge
    - [ ] General knowledge
    - [ ] Tacit knowledge
    - [x] Explicit knowledge

10. Complete the sentence: “The stages of the KMSD model are as follows: […]”.
    - [ ] Sense development, reviewing, designing, exploring, evolving.
    - [ ] Sense development, envisioning, designing, exploring, reviewing.
    - [ ] Sense-making, envisioning, designing, reviewing, evolving.
    - [x] Sense-making, envisioning, designing, exploring, evolving.
